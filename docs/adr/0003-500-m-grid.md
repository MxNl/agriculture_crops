# 3. A 500 m grid for crop maps and statistics, computed in one pass

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-17
- Code: `R/crop_change_stats.R`, `R/plot_top_crops.R`

## Context

The 10 m maps have about 7 billion cells per year. National maps cannot show
10 m pixels, and per-pixel statistics over eight years do not fit in memory.

## Decision

- Aggregate to a 500 m grid (50 × 50 pixels), snapped to multiples of 500 m.
- For the crop maps: the modal (most frequent) agricultural class per 500 m
  cell, ignoring class 0, so cells at the edge of farmland show the crop
  rather than "no farmland".
- For the statistics: per 500 m cell and year the number of 10 m pixels of
  every class and of farmland (`results/croptypes_dlr_<year>_500m_class_counts.tif`).
  The class counts keep the exact areas; a check confirms that their sums
  equal the full-resolution pixel counts.
- Compute all 500 m statistics (class counts, repeat cultivation, rotation
  transitions; ADR 4, 5) in one block-wise pass that reads one 500 m row of
  all years at a time.

## Consequences

- Statistics are exact pixel counts, only their location is aggregated to
  500 m.
- The modal class is a display choice: small crops rarely win a cell, so the
  maps show where a crop dominates; areas always come from counts (ADR 2).
- The single pass takes about an hour and needs memory for only one band of
  rows.

## Alternatives considered

- Statistics on the modal 500 m raster: rejected, minority crops would
  vanish.
- Per-year separate passes: slower and cannot compute per-pixel measures
  over years (repeat cultivation, transitions).
